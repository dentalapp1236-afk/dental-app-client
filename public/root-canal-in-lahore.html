What is a root canal?

A root canal is a treatment that saves a badly decayed or infected tooth instead of removing it. When the soft pulp inside a tooth becomes infected — often causing severe toothache or sensitivity — the dentist removes the infected pulp, cleans and disinfects the inside of the tooth, then fills and seals it. A crown is usually placed afterwards to protect the tooth.

Signs you may need a root canal

  • Persistent or severe toothache, especially when chewing
  • Lingering sensitivity to hot or cold
  • Swelling or tenderness in the nearby gum
  • A darkening tooth, or a deep cavity or cracked tooth

The procedure

Under local anaesthesia, the dentist removes the infected pulp, shapes and cleans the canals, and seals them. Most root canals are completed in one or two visits. Afterwards, a filling or crown restores the tooth's strength and appearance.

Root canal — frequently asked questions

How much does a root canal cost in Lahore?

A root canal typically costs around Rs 8,000 to Rs 20,000 for a front tooth and Rs 15,000 to Rs 35,000 for a molar. A crown afterwards is usually an additional cost.

Is a root canal painful?

The treatment is done under local anaesthesia, so it is usually no more uncomfortable than a filling. It relieves the pain caused by the infected tooth. Mild soreness for a few days afterwards is normal.

How many visits does a root canal take?

Many root canals are completed in one or two visits, depending on the tooth and the level of infection.

Do I need a crown after a root canal?

Often yes, especially for back teeth. A treated tooth can become brittle, and a crown protects it and restores full chewing strength.
