What is professional teeth whitening?

Teeth whitening is a safe, popular cosmetic treatment that lightens stains and brightens the natural colour of your teeth. A dentist applies a professional whitening gel — much stronger and safer than most over-the-counter products — to remove discolouration caused by tea, coffee, cola, smoking and ageing.

Types of teeth whitening

In-office whitening

Done at the clinic in about an hour. The dentist applies a strong whitening gel, sometimes activated with a special light, for fast, noticeable results in a single visit.

Take-home whitening kits

The dentist makes custom trays that fit your teeth and gives you a whitening gel to use at home over one to two weeks. It is gradual but convenient.

Is it safe? What to expect

When done by a qualified dentist, whitening is safe. Some people feel mild, temporary tooth sensitivity for a day or two. Results typically last from several months to a couple of years — avoiding staining foods and smoking, plus good brushing, keeps your smile brighter for longer.

Teeth whitening — frequently asked questions

How much does teeth whitening cost in Lahore?

Professional in-office teeth whitening typically costs around Rs 15,000 to Rs 40,000, while take-home kits from a dentist can be lower. The price depends on the method and the clinic.

Is professional teeth whitening safe?

Yes. When carried out by a qualified dentist, whitening is safe. Some people notice temporary tooth sensitivity for a day or two, which usually settles on its own.

How long does teeth whitening last?

Results usually last from several months to a couple of years, depending on your diet and habits. Avoiding tea, coffee, cola and smoking helps the results last longer.

What is the difference between in-office and take-home whitening?

In-office whitening is done at the clinic in about an hour and gives faster results. Take-home whitening uses custom trays and gel you apply at home over one to two weeks.
