What is a dental implant?

A dental implant is a small titanium post that acts as an artificial tooth root. It is placed into the jawbone, and once it fuses with the bone, a custom crown is fixed on top to replace a missing tooth. Implants look, feel and function like natural teeth and are the most durable way to replace one or more missing teeth.

They are a popular alternative to dentures and bridges because they do not slip, do not damage the neighbouring teeth, and help preserve the jawbone.

The implant procedure

  • Consultation & scan: the dentist examines your teeth and jawbone, often with an X-ray or CT scan, and plans the treatment.
  • Implant placement: the titanium post is placed into the jaw under local anaesthesia.
  • Healing (osseointegration): over a few months the implant fuses with the bone.
  • Crown fitting: a custom-made crown is attached to complete the new tooth.

Recovery and aftercare

Most people return to normal activities the next day. Mild swelling or soreness for a few days is normal. Good brushing, flossing and regular dental check-ups help implants last many years — often a lifetime.

Dental implants — frequently asked questions

How much does a dental implant cost in Lahore?

A single dental implant typically costs around Rs 80,000 to Rs 200,000, including the crown. The price varies with the implant brand, whether a bone graft is needed, and the clinic, so confirm the full cost at your consultation.

How long does a dental implant take?

The full process usually takes 3 to 6 months because the implant needs time to fuse with the jawbone before the final crown is fitted. The surgery itself is a short appointment.

Is getting a dental implant painful?

The procedure is done under local anaesthesia, so you should not feel pain during placement. Mild soreness or swelling for a few days afterwards is normal.

How long do dental implants last?

With good oral hygiene and regular check-ups, implants can last 15 years or more, and often a lifetime. The crown on top may need replacement over time.
