What are fixed braces?

Fixed braces are the most common and reliable way to straighten teeth. Small brackets are bonded to each tooth and connected by a thin archwire. Over regular adjustments, the wire gently guides your teeth into a straighter, better-aligned position — improving both your smile and your bite.

They are used to correct crooked teeth, crowding, gaps, and problems with how the upper and lower teeth meet. Because they are fixed in place (not removable), they work continuously and are well suited to a wide range of cases.

Types of fixed braces

Metal braces

Stainless-steel brackets and wires — the most affordable, strong and dependable option. They are highly effective for all kinds of cases and remain the most popular choice in Lahore.

Ceramic braces

Tooth-coloured or clear brackets that blend in with your teeth, so they are far less noticeable. They straighten teeth just as well as metal braces but cost more and need a little extra care to avoid staining.

How long does treatment take?

Most people wear fixed braces for about 12 to 24 months. Simple cases can be quicker, while complex alignment issues take longer. You will visit your orthodontist every few weeks for adjustments, and wear a retainer afterwards to keep your teeth in their new position.

Fixed braces — frequently asked questions

How much do fixed braces cost in Lahore?

Fixed metal braces typically cost around Rs 60,000 to Rs 150,000, while ceramic braces usually range from about Rs 120,000 to Rs 250,000. The final cost depends on the clinic, the orthodontist's experience and how complex your case is, so confirm the exact fee at your consultation.

How long does fixed braces treatment take?

Most treatments take about 12 to 24 months. Simple cases can finish sooner and complex cases take longer. Your orthodontist will estimate the timeline after examining your teeth.

What is the difference between metal and ceramic braces?

Metal braces use stainless-steel brackets and are the most affordable and durable option. Ceramic braces use tooth-coloured brackets that are less noticeable but cost more and need more care. Both straighten teeth effectively.

Do fixed braces hurt?

You may feel mild soreness for a few days after braces are fitted or adjusted, which usually settles on its own. It is a normal sign the teeth are moving and can be managed with soft foods and over-the-counter pain relief if needed.
