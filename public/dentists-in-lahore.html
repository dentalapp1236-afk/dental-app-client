How much does a dental check-up cost in Lahore?

A routine dental consultation in Lahore typically costs around Rs 1,000 to Rs 3,000. Treatments such as scaling, fillings, braces or implants vary by clinic and case, so confirm pricing with the dentist before starting treatment.

How much do braces cost in Lahore?

Fixed metal braces in Lahore typically range from about Rs 60,000 to Rs 150,000, and ceramic braces from about Rs 120,000 to Rs 250,000, depending on the clinic and complexity of the case. See our fixed braces in Lahore guide for details.
