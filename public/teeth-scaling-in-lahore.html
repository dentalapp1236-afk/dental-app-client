What is teeth scaling?

Teeth scaling is a professional dental cleaning that removes plaque and hardened tartar (calculus) from your teeth and along the gumline — build-up that everyday brushing cannot remove. After scaling, the teeth are polished smooth. Regular scaling helps prevent gum disease, bad breath, cavities and tooth loss.

Benefits of regular cleaning

  • Healthier gums and less bleeding
  • Fresher breath
  • Removal of surface stains for a cleaner look
  • Early detection of dental problems by your dentist
  • Lower risk of cavities and gum disease

How often and what to expect

Most people benefit from a cleaning every six months. Scaling is usually quick and painless; with heavy tartar you may feel mild sensitivity for a day or two, which settles quickly. It is a routine, safe treatment that protects your long-term oral health.

Teeth scaling — frequently asked questions

How much does teeth scaling cost in Lahore?

Professional teeth scaling and polishing typically costs around Rs 3,000 to Rs 8,000, depending on how much tartar has built up and the clinic.

How often should I get my teeth scaled?

Most people benefit from a professional scaling and cleaning every six months, though your dentist may recommend more or less often based on your gum health.

Does teeth scaling damage the teeth or enamel?

No. Scaling removes only plaque and tartar, not the tooth itself. It is a safe, routine cleaning that helps prevent gum disease and cavities.

Is scaling painful?

Scaling is usually painless. If there is heavy tartar or gum inflammation you may feel mild discomfort, and the dentist can use anaesthetic gel for comfort.
